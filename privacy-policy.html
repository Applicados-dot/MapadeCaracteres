Privacy Policy for Character Map

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · App version: 1.0.0

Character Map is an application designed to browse, search, and copy Unicode characters, symbols, emojis, and special characters. It has been developed following a privacy-by-design approach: it operates entirely offline and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

1. Data Controller

Developer: Applicados
Contact: infogooweb@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

  • The application does not require user registration.
  • We do not request or store names, email addresses, or passwords.
  • Search history, favorites, and settings are stored only on the user's device.
  • We do not collect usage statistics or analytics data.
  • No data is transmitted to external servers.

3. Local Storage

All information used by Character Map is stored locally on the user's device.

  • No data is sent to external servers.
  • No data is synchronized with cloud services.
  • All information remains under the exclusive control of the user.
  • The character database is included directly within the application.

4. Clipboard Access

The application allows users to copy characters and symbols to the device clipboard for use in other applications.

  • Clipboard operations occur only when explicitly requested by the user.
  • The application does not collect or transmit copied content.
  • We do not monitor how copied content is used after it leaves the application.

5. Notifications

Character Map does not use notifications and does not request permission to send them.

6. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, or personal documents.
  • We do not use tracking or telemetry tools.
  • We do not share data with third parties.
  • We do not sell user information.

7. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Character Map does not display advertisements and does not use third-party advertising networks.

The application also does not use analytics services, user tracking technologies, advertising profiling, or remote usage statistics collection.

8. Data Export and Backups

The application does not automatically create backups or export information to external services.

If users choose to back up their device, the management and protection of those backups are entirely their responsibility.

9. Children's Privacy

Character Map is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

10. Security

The application is designed to minimize data exposure by relying exclusively on local storage. However, no system can guarantee absolute security, and users are responsible for protecting physical access to their devices.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect application improvements or legal requirements. The most recent version will replace any previous versions.
